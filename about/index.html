About

Conservatory-trained musician, Army intelligence veteran, and operations planner. I play bass in a band called Mantaray, and I publish my multidisciplinary art under the name skele.t0l.

The Arc

Conservatory

Oboe performance and music education. Formal training in theory, pedagogy, and performing under pressure.

U.S. Army

Arabic linguist and intelligence analyst. Learned how to teach under duress, lead small teams, and operate inside ambiguity.

Operations Planning

Production planning and supply chain coordination in consumer goods. Translating complexity into clarity for cross-functional teams.

skele.t0l

Multidisciplinary art, frameworks, and tools. Building the connective tissue between music, language, and systems.

The Thread

Music was the foundation. I learned how to practice, how to teach, how to listen, and how to show up for a group. The conservatory taught me grit, collaboration, and how to perform under pressure before I had language for any of it.

The Army took that to a level I couldn't have imagined. Everything that made me a good musician made me a good soldier: communication, adaptability, the instinct to support whatever team you're part of. Figure out what the group needs, deliver it under pressure, hold the line when it gets chaotic.

Now I'm synthesizing both into something new. What stays consistent is the drive to bring collaboration and clarity to whatever team I'm part of, regardless of what it looks like or how it functions.
